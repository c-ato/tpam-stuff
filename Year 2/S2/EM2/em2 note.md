$\mu_{0}\sim 4\pi \times 10^{-7} \frac{H}{m}$
$\varepsilon_{0}\mu_{0}=\frac{1}{c^{2}}$

E-static field
$\bar{F}= \frac{Qq}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}} \frac{\bar{r}}{r^{3}}=\frac{Qq}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}} \frac{\bar{\hat{r}}}{r^{2}}$
$\bar{E}(\bar{r})= \frac{\bar{F}}{q}=\frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}} \frac{\bar{r}}{r^{3}} \left[ \frac{l}{m} \right]$

Conservative potential $\varphi(\bar{r})$
$\bar{E}=-\bar{\nabla}\varphi(\bar{r})$
$\varphi_{B}=\varphi_{A}-\int ^{} _{\ell} \bar{E} \, d\bar{\ell}$
$\oint_{L}\bar{E}\,d \bar{\ell}=0$

$\bar{E}=\sum_{i}\bar{E}_{i}$
$\varphi=\sum_{i}\varphi_{i}$

Gauss Law
$\int ^{}_{S} \bar{E}\cdot \, d\bar{S}=\frac{1}{\varepsilon_{0}}\int ^{}_{V} p \, dV= \frac{Q}{\varepsilon_{0}}$ - universal
Current density:
$\bar{J}=p\bar{v}$

Biot-Savart Law
$\bar{B}(\bar{r})= \frac{\mu_{0}}{4\pi} \frac{I d \bar{\ell}\times \bar{r}}{r^{3}}= \frac{m_{0}}{4\pi} \frac{(\bar{J}\times \bar{r})dV}{r^{3}}$

Magentic Force
$\bar{F}=Id \bar{\ell}\times \bar{B}$
$\int ^{}_{S} \bar{B}\cdot \, d\bar{S}=0$ - no monopoles, is universal

$\bar{B}=\sum_{i}\bar{B}_{i}$

Ampere's Law
$\oint_{L}\bar{B}\cdot d \bar{\ell}=\mu_{0}\int ^{}_{S} \bar{J}\cdot \, d\bar{S}=\mu_{0}I$

Lorentz Force 
$\bar{F}=\bar{F}_{E}+\bar{F}_{B}=q(E+\bar{v}\times \bar{B})$
