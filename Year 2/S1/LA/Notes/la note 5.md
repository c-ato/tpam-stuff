> [!definition|*]- (Semi)-definite bilinear forms
> $B$ is define if $B>0(<0) \forall v \neq \mathbf{0}$ 
> Semi-definite if $B\geq 0(\leq 0)$ 
> Otherwise is indefinite
 ^def-la-def-bi-lin-form

> [!proposition|*]- Matrix representation of bilinear form
> $$B(\underline{v}_{i},\underline{v}_{j})=\mathbf{y}^{T}G\mathbf{x}\qquad G_{ij}=B(\underline{v}_{i},\underline{v}_{j})$$
 ^prp-la-mat-rep-bilin-form

> [!definition|*]- Quadratic Form
> $Q:V\to \mathbb{F}$, $Q(\underline{v})=B(\underline{v},\underline{v})$ and is said to be induvved by $B$
 ^def-la-quad-form
