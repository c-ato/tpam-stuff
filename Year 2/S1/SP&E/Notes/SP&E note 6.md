prob of simple box situation where either on right or left with $N$ particles:
$$P(n)=\frac{^{N}C_{n}}{2^{N}}\qquad \left< n \right>  =\sum^{N}_{n=0}nP(n)$$
$$\sigma=\left[ \sum^{N}_{n=0}(n-\bar{n})^{2}P(n) \right] ^{\frac{1}{2}}=\frac{\sqrt[  ]{ N }}{2}\qquad$$
Now for density $\frac{n}{N}$ 
$$\sigma_{F}=\left[ \sum^{N}_{n=0}\left( \frac{n}{N}-\frac{\bar{n}}{N} \right)^{2}P(n) \right] ^{\frac{1}{2}}=\frac{\sigma}{N}=\frac{1}{2\sqrt[  ]{ N }}\implies \text{width}=2\sigma_{F}=\frac{1}{\sqrt[  ]{ N }}$$
For big $N$ like $N_{A}$ the width is $\sim 10^{-12}$ so only average behaviour is observed
