$\implies \frac{ \partial^{2} \Psi }{ \partial x^{2} }=\left[ \cos(kx-\omega t)+\gamma \sin(kx-\omega t)\right]$

$\alpha \frac{ \partial^{2} \Psi }{ \partial x^{2} }+V_{0}\Psi=\beta \frac{ \partial \Psi }{ \partial t }$
