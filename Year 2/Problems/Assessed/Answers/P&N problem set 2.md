SLAC discovered the structure of the proton by scattering an electron beam off a proton at a centre-of-mass energy of $6.4 GeV$. 
If the proton is at rest, what is the momentum of the electron beam, assuming the electron momentum is in the positive z-direction? 
$$M=\sqrt[  ]{ \left( \frac{E_{e}}{c^{2}}+\frac{E_{p}}{c^{2}} \right)^{2}-\left( \frac{P_{e}}{c}+\frac{P_{p}}{c} \right)^{2} }=\frac{6.4GeV}{c^{2}}$$
$$P_{p}=0\qquad E_{e}=\sqrt[  ]{ m_{e}^{2}c^{4}+P_{e}^{2}c^{2} }\qquad E_{p}=\sqrt[  ]{ m_{p}^{2}c^{4} }=m_{p}c^{2}=938MeV\qquad m_{e}c^{2}=511keV$$$$M=\sqrt[  ]{ m_{e}^{2}+\cancel{ \frac{P_{e}^{2}}{c^{2}} }+\frac{2m_{p}E_{e}}{c^{2}}+m_{p}^{2} -\cancel{ P_{e}^{2}c^{2} }}=\frac{6.4GeV}{c^{2}}$$
$$\frac{\left(6.4GeV \right)^{2}-m_{p}^{2}c^{4}-m_{e}^{2}c^{4}}{2m_{p}c^{2}}=E_{e}=21.3\dots GeV=\sqrt[  ]{ m_{e}^{2}c^{4}+P_{e}^{2}c^{2} }$$
$$P_{e}=\sqrt[  ]{ (21.3\dots GeV)^{2}-m_{e}^{2}c^{4} }\approx 21.3GeV$$
