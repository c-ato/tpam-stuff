2. Let $\mathbf{F} = (y^{3} + y, 3xy^{2})$ and let $\mathbf{r} = (x, y)$ be the position vector in $\mathbb{R}^{2}$.
(a) Is $\mathbf{F}$ a conservative field? Explain your answer.
$\mathbf{F}=(P,Q)=(y^{2}+y,3xy)$ and this field is only conservative if $\frac{ \partial P }{ \partial y }=\frac{ \partial Q }{ \partial x }$. $\frac{ \partial P }{ \partial y }=3y^{2}+1$ and $\frac{ \partial Q }{ \partial x }=3y^{2}$ and we see that $\frac{ \partial P }{ \partial y }\neq \frac{ \partial Q }{ \partial x }$ and so it is not conservative
