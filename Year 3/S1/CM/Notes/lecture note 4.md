$$\varepsilon_{ijk}=-\varepsilon_{jik}\qquad(\underline{\nabla}\times \underline{v})_{i}=\varepsilon_{ijk}\partial_{j}v_{k}$$
If $\varepsilon_{ijk}$, which is anti-symmetric with indices, is paired with something symmetric in the indices, then the sum is $0$. i.e.
$$\varepsilon_{ijk}\delta_{jk}=\varepsilon_{ijj}=0\qquad \varepsilon_{ijk}a_{j}a_{k}=0$$
$$\underline{\nabla}\cdot(\underline{\nabla}\times \underline{u})=\partial_{i}(\varepsilon_{ijk}\partial_{j}u_{k})=u_{k}\underbrace{ \varepsilon_{ijk}\partial_{i}\partial_{j} }_{ 0 }$$
