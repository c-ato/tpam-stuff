Given that $a_{k}\geq0$ and $a_{k+1}\geq 0$.
$$
a_{1}^{2}+\dots +a_{k+1}^{2}\geq 0\therefore a_{k+2}=\frac{1}{a_{1}^{2}+\dots +a_{k+1}^{2}}\geq 0
$$
$\therefore$ by induction $\forall n \in \mathbb{N}a_{n}\geq 0$  
We have shown the sequence is bounded below, we can then show the sequence converges by MCT by showing it is also a monotone sequence.

Given $a_{k}\geq a_{k+1}$
$$
\because a_{1}^{2}+\dots +a_{k+1}^{2}\geq a_{n}\geq 0 \implies a_{1}^{2}+\dots +a_{k}^{2}\therefore a_{k+2}=\frac{1}{a_{1}^{2}+\dots +a_{k+1}^{2}}\leq \frac{1}{a_{1}^{2}+\dots +a_{k}^{2}}=a_{k+1}
$$
$\therefore$ the sequence tends to $L\geq0$ so hence $a_{1}^{2}+\dots +a_{k}^{2}=\frac{1}{a_{k+1}}$ diverges to $\infty$ by lefthand side which forces $L=0$ so righthand side also diverges and we may then select an $k>N$ such that $a_{1}^{2}+\dots +a_{N}^{2}>2017^{2017}$
