
> [!definition|*]- Tangent Space
> This is a $n$-dimensional vector space that is attached at some point, $x$, of a [[Manifolds|differentiable manifold]], $M$ and is denoted $T_{x}M$. The basis for this vector space may be written from the coordinates of $x$ from a local coordinate [[Riemann Surface|chart]] $(x_{1},\dots,x_{n})$ denoted as $\left( \frac{ \partial  }{ \partial x_{1} }|_{x},\dots,\frac{ \partial  }{ \partial x_{n} }|_{x} \right)$. We can then write for $v \in T_{x}M$ as $v= \sum_{i=1}^{n} v_{i} \frac{ \partial  }{ \partial x_{i} }|_{x}$
> 
> This is a generalisation of the tangent line for $n$-dimensions - tangent line for [[Homotogy|curve]], tangent plane for [[surfaces]] etc.
