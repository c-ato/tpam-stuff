> [!definition|*]- Equivariant
> Let $G$ be a group, $X$ and $Y$ be sets on which $G$ acts, and a function $f:X\to Y$. $f$ is then defined to be equivariant if the following is satisfied - that is if it preserves the group action, $f(x\cdot g)=f(x)\cdot g\forall g \in G,\forall x \in X$. 
 ^def-alg-equivar
