> [!definition|*]- Equivariant
> Let $G$ be a group, $X=U\times G$ and $Y=W\times G$, where $U$ and $W$ are two sets, and a function $f:X\to Y$. $F$ is then defined to be equivariant if the following is satisfied - that is if it preserves the group action, $f(x\cdot g)=f(x)\cdot g\forall g \in G,\forall x \in X$.
