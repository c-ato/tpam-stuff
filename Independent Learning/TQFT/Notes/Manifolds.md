> [!definition|*]- Manifold
> This is a [[Topology|topological]] space, $X$, that locally resembles Euclidean space near each point, or more formally:
> For each $x \in O_{X} \subset  X \exists f:O_{X}\to O_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$ where $x$ is an arbitrary point, $O$ is a [[Neighbourhoods|neighbourhood]] in its respective space and $n$ is the dimension of $X$.
 ^def-top-mani

> [!definition|*]- Smooth (Manifold)
> This means that a manifold is infinitely differentiable after it is locally mapped by a [[Riemann Surface|chart]] to a Euclidean space.
 ^def-cat-mani-smoo
