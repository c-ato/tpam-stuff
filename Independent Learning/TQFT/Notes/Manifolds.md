> [!definition|*]- Manifold
> This is a [[Topology|topological]] space, $X$, that locally resembles Euclidean space near each point, or more formally:
> For each $x \in O_{X} \subset  X \exists f:O_{X}\to O_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$ where $x$ is an arbitrary point, $O$ is a [[Neighbourhoods|neighbourhood]] in its respective space, $f$ is a chart to $\mathbb{R}^{n}$, and $n$ is the dimension of $X$.
 ^def-top-mani

> [!definition|*]- Smooth (Manifold)
> This is a differential manifold that is infinitely differentiable
 ^def-cat-mani-smoo

> [!definition|*]- Differentiable Manifold
> Let $M$ be a manifold, where charts, $\phi:M\to \mathbb{R}^{n}$, and transition maps, $\phi_{i}\circ\phi_{j}^{-1}: \phi_{j}(U_{i}\cap U_{j})\to \phi_{i}(U_{i}\cap U_{j})$, exist and the transition map is required to be differentiable
