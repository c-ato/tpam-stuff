> [!definition|*]- First Countable Spaces
> Let $X$ be a topological space, and $x \in X$ such that there is a countable amount of neighbourhoods for each $x$.
 ^def-top-1st-count-spa

> [!definition|*]- Second Countable Spaces
> Let $X$ be a topological space. It is second countable if there exists some countable collection $\mathcal{U} = \{ U_{i} \}^{\infty}_{i=1}$ of open subsets of $X$ such that any open subset of $T$ can be written as a union of elements of some subfamily of $\mathcal{U}$.
 ^def-top-2nd-count-spa

> [!theorem|*]- Second Countable are First Countable Spaces
> Second countable $\implies$ first countable.
 ^thm-2nd-impl-1st-count-spa

> [!definition|*]- A more Complex Surface Definition.
> A surface $S$ is a connected, second countable, Hausdorff topological space with a family of homomorphisms $φ_{\alpha} : U_{\alpha} → D_{\alpha}$ from domains $U_{\alpha}$ that form an open cover of $S$ to open subsets $D_{\alpha}$ in the complex plane. 
 ^def-top-riem-surf

> [!definition|*]- Charts and Atlas
> The homeomorphisms of a Riemann Surface are called charts, and the family they belong to is called an atlas $A$ on $S$. We write $A = \{(φ_{\alpha}, U_{\alpha})\}$.
